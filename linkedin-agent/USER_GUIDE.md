# User Guide

## Onboarding
1. Sign in and connect your LinkedIn via the "Connect LinkedIn" button.
2. Grant requested permissions on LinkedIn.
3. The app will pull your profile to analyze strengths, skills, and brand voice.

## Create Your Strategy
1. Open Strategy → Generate content pillars and a 4-week calendar.
2. Adjust topics, frequency, and tone to match your brand.

## Generate Content
1. Use the Composer to generate posts (text, carousel, article, poll).
2. Edit, refine, and run Optimize to improve hooks, CTAs, and hashtags.
3. Schedule posts to your calendar.

## Monitor & Improve
1. View Analytics dashboard for reach, likes, comments, shares.
2. Run A/B tests on hooks or visuals.
3. Iterate based on what works best.

## Ethics & Compliance
- Posts are tailored to be professional, non-misleading, and inclusive.
- You can enable stricter filters in Settings.
