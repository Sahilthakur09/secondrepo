# User Guide

## Getting Started
1. Sign in and connect your LinkedIn account (mocked in this build).
2. Provide your profile details or allow the app to import from LinkedIn.
3. Review suggested brand voice and content pillars.
4. Generate content drafts and schedule posts.

## Content Creation
- Choose format (text, article, carousel, poll)
- Provide a topic or let AI propose trends
- Review hashtags and CTA suggestions

## Calendar
- Visualize scheduled posts
- Reschedule by drag-and-drop (frontend feature)

## Analytics
- Track engagement metrics over time
- Run A/B tests by posting controlled variants

## Ethics & Compliance
- Ensure posts align with professional standards
- Avoid sharing confidential information
